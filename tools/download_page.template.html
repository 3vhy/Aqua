What’s inside

  • A panel menu with a search box, a settings card for every module, and Friends, Theme and Config tabs
  • A glass interface: the HUD, pause menu, title screen, tooltips and even Minecraft’s own buttons and chat
  • Fern-style HUD with a drag-and-drop editor
  • Wardrobe: original 3D skins, wings, hats, capes, knives and fantasy swords, emotes, kill effects and a pet
  • Hit and damage effects, swing and player trails, nebula and aurora skies, a staff list
  • Music player for your own .ogg files and the song playing in Spotify, with synced lyrics found automatically
  • Waypoints, chat macros, Discord status, Color Grading, Motion Blur, Custom FOV and glint, Low Health Warning
  • Zoom, Freelook, Name Protect, Skin Change, screenshot notifications, profiles and themes

No cheats: no combat, movement, x-ray, ESP or other unfair features. Cosmetics are only visible to you. Some servers do not allow freelook; check each server’s rules before you play.

Sodium, Iris and Mod Menu are optional and were tested alongside Aqua.

Install

  1. Install Fabric Loader for 1.21.11 with the Fabric installer.
  2. Start the fabric-loader-1.21.11 profile once, then close the game.
  3. Put Fabric API and the Aqua jar in your mods folder.
  4. Start the Fabric profile and press Right Shift.

Where is my mods folder?

Windows%APPDATA%\.minecraft\mods
