## Decision key

* **Include** – implemented as observed (re-branded).
* **Adapt** – implemented, but changed to remove an unfair advantage, an external
  service, or third-party IP. The change is stated in the reason column.
* **Exclude** – deliberately not implemented (cheat, automation, hidden information,
  external service, server-specific, or someone else's IP).
* **Unclear** – not enough visible detail to identify the behaviour; noted here
  instead of guessing.
* **Video edit** – overlay added in a video editor, not client software.

Status column:
* **Done**: implemented, and the module or page was enabled, rendered and disabled in a real
  Minecraft 1.21.11 client by the automated test suite. The suite ran in the development
  environment, in the production environment with the release jar, and with Sodium, Iris and Mod Menu
  installed (see `TESTING.md`). Screenshots are in `docs/screenshots/`.
* **n/a**: excluded on purpose.
* **not implemented**: unclear in the references; the reason is given in the row.

## 5. Excluded cheats and services (complete list)

All of the following appeared in R1–R4 or S1 and are **not** implemented in any form
(no disabled toggles, no hidden code):

* **Combat automation:** Kill Aura, Triggerbot, Aim Assist / "Aimassist", Shield Breaker,
  Sprint Reset, Auto Crystal, Crystal Aura, Crystal Macro, Anchor Macro, Anchor Aura,
  Double Anchor, Auto Totem, Inv Totem, Auto Mace, Spear Mace Double Tap, Wind Charge
  Aura, Windcharge Throw, Wind Charge Jump, Pearl Throw, Pearl Catch, Key Lunge,
  Sword PvP Bot, Auto Riptide, Auto Tool.
* **Reach / hit manipulation:** Reach, Spear Reach, Piercing, Hitbox, Backtrack, Movement Fix.
* **Prediction:** Mace Prediction, pearl trajectory/landing prediction (R2 0:03 ring).
* **Movement:** Elytra Glide, Elytra Autopilot.
* **Hidden information:** Block Entity Finder, Storage ESP, Amethyst ESP, Player ESP,
  Target ESP, Chams, Nametags (through walls / with opponent gear), tracers (R1, R4 1:45),
  Sus Chunk Finder (v1/v2), Tunnel Base Finder, Media Base Finder, Cave Analyzer,
  Spawner Nametags, SpawnerBeam, Block Entity ESP, Radar with entities, FreeCam
  (R1 0:12, R3 0:06), Camera Clip, Fullbright, No Water Fog, Anti Vanish,
  Target HUD with opponent health. (The Staff List was later added on request; it only
  reads the tab list and cannot find hidden staff.)
* **Server / anticheat tooling:** Anticheat Alerts (R4 0:46, 1:20), Goliath RTP, Region
  Map, Home Reset, Tp Directions, Schematic Builder (auto-build), Fake Player.
* **External services:** Discord Presence, 67 Chat, community config server,
  "Claim your username" website. (Spotify is now shown by reading the local app, not through an
  online Spotify service; see section 8.) (The only online lookups Aqua makes are ones you ask
  for: Skin Protect fetches the skin of the name you type from Mojang, and the music player looks
  up synced lyrics on LRCLIB by title, artist and length. The lyrics lookup can be switched off.)
* **Third-party IP:** 3D character skins of SpongeBob, Charizard, Batman, Bonnie (FNAF),
  Skibidi Toilet, Tung Tung Tung Sahur, Ballerina Cappuccina, Rico, the "Six Seven"
  mascot; Fern / 67 / Clarity logos and names.
